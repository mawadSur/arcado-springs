Why this site holds 41 homes

4535–4541 Arcado Rd SW, Lilburn GA 9.44 ac deeded 236′–246′ wide × 1,754′ deep single access 2026-09-20

R-2 zoning permits up to 8 dwellings per acre — about 75 homes on 9.44 acres. The filed plan plans 41. This page explains why that gap is not caution: the parcel itself will not hold more, and the arrangement shown is the only one the ordinance allows.

The parcel is a ribbon — 236 feet wide at the road, 1,754 feet deep, with a single entrance and no other street frontage. The zoning ordinance sets a minimum lot depth of 100 feet for every dwelling. Two rows of homes therefore consume 200 feet, leaving 36 feet for the road and everything in it.

A single lane down the middle fits that in 35.9 feet, with one inch to spare. That one measurement determines the whole plan: two rows of homes, one lane between them, and no room for a third row, a wider street, or a second drive.

The governing standards

Three numbers set the capacity

Zoning Ordinance 2023-603, Table 4.1 — cottage home dwelling, R-2

Minimum lot width 50 feet · minimum lot depth 100 feet · minimum rear yard 20 feet. Section 402-3 makes the table mandatory (“dimensional requirements shall be as provided in Table 4.1”), and the standards attach to the dwelling rather than to a platted lot — so they apply however the land is owned or subdivided.

The 50-foot width is what caps the count. Usable frontage divided by 50 gives the number of homes, and no choice about the houses changes it. The single provision in the code that reduces lot width — Section 310, down to 35 feet — applies only inside an Overlay District or a redevelopment Character Area. This parcel is in neither; its Comprehensive Plan character area is “Established Residential.”

Why the homes are this size

A 50-foot lot with 5-foot side yards leaves 40 feet of buildable width. The filed plans use 38 feet of it. Making the houses smaller would not add a single home — the lot width is fixed — so the plan uses the width it has.

Both plans are single storey with zero-step entries, 36-inch doors and 5-foot turning circles in the baths, which is what the 55-and-over programme requires and what drives the depth.

The conclusion

Two 100-foot lot depths plus a 35.9-foot lane consume 235 feet 11 inches of a 236-foot width. Perimeter drives serving each row would need 44 feet where 36 feet exists. The filed arrangement is not one option among several — it is the only one this parcel and this ordinance permit.

What would change it

Three routes, all outside this application

A different dwelling type. Attached homes carry different Table 4.1 dimensions and would raise the count materially. They are also what the neighbourhood objected to, and they are not what is proposed here.

A variance on lot width. Section 1005 requires unnecessary hardship. A conforming 41-lot plan demonstrably fits this parcel, so a request for narrower lots would be a self-created hardship — the weakest possible ground.

A text amendment. Changing the cottage-home dimensions in Table 4.1 is a legislative act, not a rezoning, and a far larger undertaking than this application.
