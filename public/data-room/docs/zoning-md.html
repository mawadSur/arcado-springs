Zoning Roadmap — City of Lilburn, R-1 → MU

Jurisdiction (settled question)

City of Lilburn — not unincorporated Gwinnett. Triple-confirmed 7/25/2026 (+ independent adversarial re-verification, ~95% confidence): 1. Lilburn's official street list (rev. 2/13/2024) puts 4535/4541 Arcado Rd SW among "Addresses in City" (Arcado Rd SW, Killian Hill → Rockbridge). The road itself is county-maintained, which is why the corridor "feels" unincorporated — and why Gwinnett DOT still controls driveways/turn lanes. 2. Gwinnett 2026 tax digest: all four parcels are tax district 7 "LILBURN", ZONEDESC "R1-Single Family Residence LIL". 3. The city itself docketed RZ-2025-01 on these parcels under city R-1 zoning.

Approval body: Lilburn Planning Commission (advisory) → Mayor & City Council (final). Gwinnett's remaining roles: sewer capacity (DWR), road access (DOT), schools (GCPS), tax records.

Current zoning: Lilburn R-1 (all 4 parcels)

Single-family detached only; min lot 9,000 sf; min width 80 ft; max 4 du/ac gross; min house 1,400 sf; max height 40 ft; 50-ft front setback on a collector. Nothing in the Arcado Springs program is permitted under R-1.

Target district: MU Mixed-Use (Ord. 2023-603, §406 + §736)

  • Max gross residential density 12 du/ac (multi-family component up to 18 du/ac). 104 units / 9.44 ac = 11.0 du/ac — compliant. (On our 2.99 ac alone: not viable at scale.)
  • Min project size 2 ac. 20-ft buffer along abutting R-1/R-2 lines (Legends of Parkview).
  • Open space must include a centralized amenity area, recorded as common area.
  • Table 4.6 mix requirements — THE compliance trap: residential land 15–70%; non-residential land 15–70%; each use 30–70% of gross floor area. As filed in 2025: ~24,000 sf commercial vs ~176,800 sf residential (104 × ~1,700 sf) ≈ 12% commercial GFA → appears to FAIL the 30% floor. The refiled program must be rebalanced (more commercial, fewer/smaller units, or negotiated interpretation). Resolve at Gate B with attorney + staff. ⚠️ VERIFY interpretation at pre-app.
  • Concurrency phasing (§736): ≤50% of residential COs before 25% of non-res floor area permitted; ≤75% before 50%.
  • Submittal must include a Development Summary Report + Master Concept Plan sealed by a PE, architect, RLS, land planner, or landscape architect; once approved, both become binding zoning conditions running with the land (changes = Change-in-Conditions case, $500).

The legal weak point — MU applicability / plan consistency

  • §406-1: MU is "specifically applicable" to the Old Town Center Overlay and Lawrenceville Highway Overlay character areas. The site is in neither — it sits in "Established Residential" (2024 Comp Plan map, p.29, verified visually ⚠️).
  • §203's compatibility table maps Established Residential to R-1/R-2 only.
  • Our two textual hooks:
  • §406-1: "…All properties within these Character Areas and others that support a mix of highly compatible uses within a single development, are eligible to apply."
  • Established Residential text: higher density "will generally not be encouraged … However, infill mixed use development adjacent to residential areas will likely improve home values, and thus is an acceptable use."
  • Review criteria we must brief (§1003-7): (A) suitability vs adjacent uses; (B) adverse effect on adjacent property; (C) reasonable economic use as zoned; (D) burden on streets/utilities/schools; (E) conformity with the comprehensive plan ← the fight; (F) changed conditions.
  • Supporting context for (F): Killian Hill Rd widening 2→5 lanes between Arcado Rd and Church St (~$25M); comp plan CWP item L.06 ("Enforce Mixed Use District amendments. Draft a stand alone High Density Residential zoning district"); T.02 signalization study at Killian Hill/Church/Poplar.

Strategy options (decide at Gate B with counsel)

Option Mechanics Trade-off
A. Straight MU refile Lean on the two textual hooks + heavy conditions Fastest; carries the §406-1/§203 consistency risk into a contested hearing
B. Comp-plan amendment first/parallel Ask staff to move the site to a mixed-use-supportive designation — the city just ran a mid-cycle plan update in 2026 (closeout hearing was on the 6/25/2026 PC agenda ⚠️ check if still open) Cleanest legal footing; timing depends on the city's plan cycle
C. R-2 fallback R-2 allows ~8 du/ac, townhomes/cottages/duplexes; explicitly the "transition" district next to MU areas Kills the commercial program; salvages residential value if MU is unwinnable
D. Hybrid ask File MU; signal openness to Council approving a lesser district (§1003-9 lets Council approve a different classification) Keeps every door open at the hearing

Process mechanics (2026)

  • Pre-application conference ~1 week before submittal, "highly recommended" — Reid Turner, 770-279-3715, returner@cityoflilburn.com.
  • File at City Hall, 340 Main St; site plan PDF also emailed to Turner. Fee $1,250 (5.0–9.9 ac).
  • Flow: staff analysis + written recommendation → PC hearing, 4th Thursday 7:30 PM (advisory) → Council hearing, 2nd Monday 7:30 PM (final). Applicant represented at both.
  • Legal notice: Gwinnett Daily Post ad ≥15 days before PC, ≥30 days before Council; mailed notice to adjoining owners; on-site sign ≥15 days prior. RZ-2025-01 died on a defective ad — audit the city's notices ourselves this time (tear sheets + sign photos in LOG.md).
  • Withdrawal allowed only until the Council-hearing legal ad runs; after that the case MUST go to a final Council action. An advertised case cannot be quietly pulled.
  • Applicant-requested tabling after advertisement: 50% of original fee.
  • 12-month reapplication bar applies only after Council denial (6-month floor; waivable by Mayor & Council). Withdrawn ≠ denied → RZ-2025-01 likely leaves us clear. ⚠️ VERIFY (Gate 0.1).

Adjacent regulatory gates (not city zoning, still mandatory)

  • Gwinnett DWR sewer capacity certification: signed/sealed request + GIS tie-in map → DWRCapacityCertification@GwinnettCounty.com (678-376-7026); availability letters 678-376-7139. A 20-ft sanitary sewer easement already crosses the NW boundary (per CAD plan) — likely tie-in.
  • Gwinnett DOT (Arcado Rd is county ROW): TIS per county guidelines (Level 2 expected at ~90–140 peak-hour trips; GA PE seal required), driveway permit, turn-lane commitments. The site plan already contemplates one consolidated managed driveway + a left-turn pocket — make these formal voluntary conditions; they're also the best traffic-objection answers.
  • Environmental: GA 25-ft state-waters buffer; Lilburn stream buffer: 50-ft undisturbed + 25-ft additional impervious setback; FEMA floodplain must be mapped on the MCP; wetlands per ACOE.
  • DRI: not triggered (thresholds: >400 units / >400k sf / >120 ac — we're far below). No ARC review.
  • Later stages: §1106 site & design review (landscape/tree plan, elevations, materials board), LDP ($40/disturbed ac + NPDES $80/ac), building permits ($7/$1,000 valuation + 2% public-art fee).
