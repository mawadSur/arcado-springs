The age restriction the paragraph offers is the one in voluntary condition 1: housing for older persons under 42 U.S.C. §3607(b)(2)(C) and 24 CFR §§100.300–.308, which requires at least 80 % of occupied units to have a resident aged 55 or older. At 41 units, 0.80 × 41 = 32.8, and because the fractional unit counts into the 80 % under 24 CFR §100.305(g), at least 33 of the 41 units must be occupied by at least one person aged 55 or older (33 ÷ 41 = 80.5 %; 32 units would be 78.0 % and would fail). Eight units carry no age requirement. The recorded declaration states that figure and recomputes it if the unit count changes.
