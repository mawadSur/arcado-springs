verify/notice/ — legal-notice evidence

0. Citation convention — whole document

Every bare "§" in this index is to the Lilburn Zoning Ordinance, Ord. 2023-603; "Instructions" is the City of Lilburn 2026 Application Instructions — Rezoning/SUP/CIC (p. 2 carries the hearing schedule and the legal-notice paragraph). Every other authority — O.C.G.A. §36-67A-3(B) — is named in full where it appears.

1. Why this folder exists

RZ-2025-01 — the 2025 mixed-use application on this same property — was TABLED by the Planning Commission on 2025-08-28 because of "a clerical error in the legal advertisement for the public hearing via the Gwinnett Daily Post advertisement and on-site sign advertisement" (Planning Commission minutes, status/rz-2025-01/2025-08-28_PC-minutes-item4709.pdf). The case was never heard on its merits. A filing cycle was lost to a notice defect that nobody could disprove after the fact, because nobody had kept the ad text, the tear sheets or dated photographs of the sign.

Under Lilburn Zoning Ordinance 2023-603 §1003-6, the City — not the applicant — publishes the advertisement, posts the sign and mails the letters. The applicant nevertheless bears the whole cost of a defect: a tabled case loses the cycle. So the applicant's job is to (a) review the notice before it goes out and (b) keep an independent, dated copy of every element as it goes out. That is what this folder is.

Two things this folder is not: it is not a substitute for the City's own file, and it does not make a defective notice good. If any element is wrong or late, tell the Planning Director the same day — the remedy is to re-notice for the next cycle, not to hold a hearing on a defective notice.

Two procedural consequences worth writing on the front of the folder:

  • A tabling is not a denial. Ord. 2023-603 §1003-11's 12-month refiling bar runs from a denial; RZ-2025-01 was tabled, so the bar does not apply (confirm in writing — pre-application question Q10, verify/preapp-notes.md).
  • There is no withdrawal after the Council advertisement runs. Ord. 2023-603 §1003-10. In Cycle A the single advertisement of 09/29/2026 covers both hearings, so the last practical day to withdraw is before 09/29/2026 (Cycle B: before 11/03/2026). No fee refund once the case is scheduled.

3. File-naming convention

N<step>-<YYYY-MM-DD>_<slug>.<ext>
  • N<step> — the notice step from docs/10 §2.2 (N1 … N9), so the folder sorts into the order the notice actually happened.
  • <YYYY-MM-DD> — the date of the event the artefact records (the date the ad ran, the day the sign was photographed, the date on the mailed letter), never the date the file was saved.
  • <slug> — lower-case, hyphenated, no spaces.
  • <ext> — pdf for anything printed or issued by the City or the publisher; jpg for photographs (keep the camera original with its EXIF timestamp intact — do not crop, rotate or re-compress the filed copy); png for screenshots; md for the worksheets computed here.

Sequence numbers for repeats: -01, -02 (N4-2026-10-14_sign-photo-02.jpg). If a second cycle is used, add the cycle letter before the slug: N2-2026-11-03_cycleB-gdp-tear-sheet-1.pdf.

Keep index.md in this folder as a running log — one line per artefact: date, file name, what it is, who obtained it, and anything that looked wrong. Two copies of everything: this folder and a paper folder for the attorney (step N9).

4.2 What each artefact is, and what to check on it

N1 — draft advertisement text, obtained from the Planning Director before publication. File the draft itself, plus N1-2026-09-25_ad-text-request-email.pdf (the request) and N1-2026-09-28_ad-text-review-notes.md (what was checked, by whom, and any correction sent back). Check every §1003-6.1 element against the draft: the time, place and purpose of each hearing; all four addresses — 4535, 4537, 4539 and 4541 Arcado Rd SW; PINs R6123 033 / 015 / 014 / 162; Land Lot 123, 6th District; acreage 9.44; present zoning R-1; proposed zoning R-2; both hearing dates and times; and the case number. This is the step that did not happen in 2025.

N2a and N2b — two Gwinnett Daily Post tear sheets. The whole page as printed, showing the masthead, the date of issue and the page number, with the notice legible. Buy two: keep N2a flat and unmarked as the clean copy, and use N2b for the mark-up. File the comparison as N2-2026-09-29_ad-vs-draft-comparison.md — is the published wording identical to N1, word for word, yes or no.

N2c — the publisher's affidavit of publication. The sworn proof of publication that the Gwinnett Daily Post issues and the City files. Request a copy from the City in the week the advertisement runs; do not wait until the hearing.

N3 — day-count computation from publication to each hearing. Worked and signed: the publication date, each hearing date, the count in days, and the window each count must fall in — 15 to 45 days before the Planning Commission (§1003-6.1) and at least 30 days before the City Council (Instructions p. 2). Use the worksheet in §6; state the counting convention used.

N4 — dated photographs of the posted sign, on the day of posting and weekly thereafter. Each set: one wide shot showing the sign in place on the Arcado Rd frontage with the road visible for context; one square-on shot in which every line of the sign text is legible; one shot of a phone or newspaper showing the date if the camera has no reliable timestamp. Keep the EXIF intact. Photograph again after any storm and immediately after any replacement. Log every set in index.md.

N5 — day count from posting and mailing to the Planning Commission hearing. Fifteen days is the bare minimum in both cycles, so this count has no slack. If the sign is not up by the end of the posting day, email the Director that evening.

N6 — the mailed adjoining-owner letter and the mailing-list check. Request a copy of the letter from the City with its mailing date, and check the City's mailing list line by line against docs/06-adjoining-property-owners.md: 31 privately owned adjoining parcels (14 NE / 14 SW / 3 NW), all zoned R-1 City of Lilburn, plus the Gwinnett County Arcado Road right-of-way = 32 notice recipients, plus the owners of record of the four subject parcels. Record each name, PIN and mailing address as mailed, and flag every difference from docs/06. Re-run tools/adjoiners.py on the filing date first — ownership changes.

N7 — screenshots of both meeting agendas. From the City's Agenda Center, with the URL and the date in frame, showing the case listed under the right case number, address and zoning request. Save the agenda PDF alongside (N7-2026-10-15_pc-agenda.pdf).

N8 — hearing attendance record. Who attended for the applicant, what was presented, what was asked, and what was said about notice. Attach the sign-in sheet or the agenda annotated at the meeting.

N9 — transmittal of this folder to the attorney before each hearing. What was sent, and confirmation of receipt. File here as well the Director's written confirmation that RZ-2025-01 was tabled and not denied (pre-application question Q10) and, if staff treats RZ-2025-01 as pending, the §1003-10 withdrawal letter and its receipt.

index.md — the running log. One line per artefact: date, file name, what it is, who obtained it, and anything that looked wrong.

If any element is wrong or late: email the Planning Director the same day, keep the email in this folder as N<step>-<date>_defect-notice-to-director.pdf, and ask in writing whether the case will be re-noticed. Do not let a hearing proceed on a notice you believe is defective — that is exactly what cost the 2025 cycle.

6. Day-count worksheet (N3 and N5)

Counting convention used below: the day after the event through the day of the hearing, inclusive — the conservative reading of "at least 15 days prior to." State the convention on the worksheet; if the City counts differently, record its count too and use the smaller of the two.

From To Days Window it must fall in Result
Ad published Tue 09/29/2026 PC hearing Thu 10/22/2026 23 15–45 (§1003-6.1); ≥ 15 (Instructions) inside the window
Ad published Tue 09/29/2026 Council hearing Mon 11/09/2026 41 15–45 (§1003-6.1); ≥ 30 (Instructions) inside the window

Arithmetic, shown: 09/29 → 09/30 is 1 day, + 22 days of October to 10/22 = 23. 09/29 → 09/30 is 1 day, + 31 days of October + 9 days of November = 41. 10/07 → 10/22 = 15. 10/07 → 10/31 is 24 days, + 9 = 33.

The two counts that can fail. Both 15-day counts are exactly at the minimum. If the sign goes up or the letters go out one day late, the Planning Commission notice is defective and the case should be re-noticed rather than heard. Check the posting on the morning of 10/07 (Cycle A) or 11/11 (Cycle B), and if it has not happened by the end of that day, email the Director that evening.
